# BTL Internet Website Design Brief

## Design read
A national-level Bangladeshi ISP experience for rural, semi-urban and urban customers: dependable, human, technically credible, and easy enough for a first-time broadband customer to use on a low-cost phone.

## Concept spine
**The Living Fiber Route.** The site follows one continuous fiber connection as it leaves a network node, crosses village and town, reaches homes and businesses, and becomes a visible digital lifeline. Every major interaction behaves like a waypoint on that route.

## Delivery tier
**Cinema.** The homepage is anchored by one scroll-driven film with semantic chapters over it, surrounded by restrained premium UI and practical portal surfaces.

## Animation mode
Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey shape
**single-shot.** One continuous 15-second move is the right fit because the story is one connection traveling through one coherent Bangladesh landscape rather than unrelated worlds.

## Journey
1. **Connection begins**
   - Focal point: a clean fiber distribution node at the edge of a green rural road.
   - Headline: "ইন্টারনেট, আপনার কাছেই"
   - Copy: "দ্রুত ও নির্ভরযোগ্য ফাইবার সংযোগ, ঘর থেকে ব্যবসা পর্যন্ত।"
2. **Fiber reaches home**
   - Focal point: the glowing fiber path flows past homes and a family using a laptop.
   - Headline: "ঘরের জন্য নির্ভরযোগ্য নেট"
   - Copy: "পড়াশোনা, কাজ, বিনোদন এবং প্রতিদিনের অনলাইন জীবনের জন্য।"
3. **Local business stays connected**
   - Focal point: the same route reaches shops and a small office cluster.
   - Headline: "ব্যবসা চলুক সংযোগে"
   - Copy: "SME, অফিস এবং কর্পোরেট প্রয়োজনের জন্য স্থিতিশীল সংযোগ।"
4. **Coverage grows**
   - Focal point: the camera rises to reveal village, town and a restrained city horizon linked by the same line.
   - Headline: "এলাকা বাড়ে, সংযোগও বাড়ে"
   - Copy: "ফাইবার, পার্টনার এবং রিসেলার নেটওয়ার্কে বিস্তৃত কভারেজ।"
5. **BTL Internet resolves**
   - Focal point: route converges into a clean BTL-style connected node form with open sky and negative space.
   - Headline: "সহজ সংযোগ, প্রতিদিন"
   - Copy: "কভারেজ দেখুন, প্যাকেজ বাছুন, নতুন সংযোগ নিন।"

## World grammar
- Perspective: human eye-level rural road that gradually rises to a wider regional view.
- Palette: Deep Navy #011F43, Dark Blue #042A5D, Electric Blue #0289E3, Bright Cyan #09C0F9, Green accent #2DC455, Slate #47576E, White #FFFFFF.
- Light: bright soft daylight from camera-left, locked exposure and neutral white balance.
- Surface finish: realistic Bangladesh landscape with clean premium telecom art direction, not stock-ad gloss.
- Fiber behavior: one elegant electric-blue/cyan line with small connected nodes, never heavy neon.
- Background behavior: detailed enough to feel real, center-safe and calm enough for readable HTML copy.
- Film rule: one unbroken steady move, no cuts, no shake, no text, no logo in footage.

## Mobile framing
Keep every human, network node and fiber focal point inside the central 55% of frame. Mobile uses a lighter encode and tighter center crop, while chapter copy stacks above or below the visual safe zone.

## Delivery budget
Desktop scrub film total target: <= 32 MiB. Mobile scrub film total target: <= 16 MiB.

## Locked palette
The explicit BTL Internet brand palette is locked: #011F43 / #042A5D / #0289E3 / #09C0F9 / optional #2DC455 / #47576E / #FFFFFF. White is the dominant canvas, navy is the trust anchor, electric blue is the single dominant interaction accent, and green is used only for status semantics.

## Locked type
- English and numerals: Inter.
- Bangla: Noto Sans Bengali.
- Display hierarchy: bold Inter with restrained tracking; Bangla headlines use Noto Sans Bengali SemiBold/Bold.
- The user explicitly specified these brand fonts, so they override the generic display-font preference.

## Combinatorial design pick
- Theme paradigm: Pristine Light.
- Background character: full-bleed cinematic imagery inside the journey, clean white surfaces elsewhere.
- Typography character: Swiss rational sans with hard hierarchy.
- Hero architecture: massive image-first with restrained text.
- Section system: Swiss grid discipline with deliberate asymmetric breaks.
- Signature components: vertical rhythm lines, off-grid editorial block, oversized metrics strip, layered image crop frames.
- Narrative spine: journey / waypoints.
- Second-read moment: one asymmetric bleed in the reseller section.

## Section plan
1. Sticky navigation + scroll-scrub journey: cinematic image canvas with restrained chapter copy.
2. Coverage checker: wide utility strip with map preview and status result.
3. Packages: horizontal plan rail with one emphasized plan, not equal three-card blocks.
4. Why BTL: oversized metric/benefit strip with divided rows and custom icon set.
5. Coverage map: full-width cartographic panel plus searchable area list.
6. Home internet: layered lifestyle image crop with stacked copy.
7. Business internet: editorial table/list with one technical image plate.
8. Reseller: asymmetric bleed panel with wholesale/reseller workflow.
9. Connection process: vertical waypoint timeline.
10. Customer reviews: split testimonial wall, compact and believable.
11. Support: utility hub with issue categories and ticket CTA.
12. Footer: deep navy information field.

## Portal surfaces
Customer, reseller and admin dashboards use the same brand tokens but shift to denser functional layouts. They prioritize status, billing, support, coverage and operations over marketing visuals.

## CTA inventory
- Hero primary: **নতুন সংযোগ** as a filled electric-blue rectangular control with a directional edge motion.
- Hero secondary: **Coverage Check** as an underlined text action with moving node marker.
- Package action: **Choose Plan** as a plan-attached bottom rail control.
- Coverage action: **See Coverage** as a map-corner tab.
- Business action: **Business Contact** as a framed navy block.
- Reseller action: **Become a Reseller** as an asymmetric banner action.
- Support action: **Open Ticket** as a compact utility control with status-line animation.

## Asset plan
- One 6-panel storyboard for the continuous fiber journey.
- One final scroll-scrub film + desktop/mobile encodes + exact-frame posters.
- Two still hero/section candidates in the same rural-to-urban world for non-video fallbacks and route pages.
- Two atmospheric section plates.
- Lifestyle imagery for home and business sections.
- One consistent 8-glyph custom icon sheet.
- Existing BTL Internet brand identity direction honored; no replacement brand concept.
- Full favicon/head kit.
- 1200x630 OG image and 3:2 community cover in the same visual system.

## Interaction principles
- Motion communicates route progress, hierarchy, feedback or state only.
- Reduced-motion users get static posters and immediate content.
- No content relies on hover.
- No section begins invisible in screenshots.
- Forms use labels above fields and errors below.
- Mobile new-connection CTA remains easy to reach.
